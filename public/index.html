Getting Started

Three simple steps

No passwords, no Instagram login. Just your username and you're all set.

1

Open the Bot

Click "Start on Telegram" to open @unfollowalert_bot and hit /start.

2

Set Your Username

Send /set yourusername with your Instagram handle. That's the only setup needed.

3

Get Notified

We check every 12 hours and send you a detailed report of who unfollowed and who's new.
